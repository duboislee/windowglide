Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The app

WindowGlide runs entirely on your Mac. It has no account and no analytics, and it does not send window titles, thumbnails or usage data anywhere. Window thumbnails are kept in memory only while the switcher is open and are never saved.

License keys are verified on your Mac, without contacting a server.

Update checks

WindowGlide checks for updates by downloading an update list from GitHub (github.io / github.com). Like any web request, this exposes your IP address and app version to GitHub. You can turn automatic checks off in the update prompt.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Paddle.com as Merchant of Record. Paddle processes your name, email address, billing country and payment details under Paddle's privacy policy. We receive your email address and order number so we can send and support your license key, and we keep them only for that purpose.

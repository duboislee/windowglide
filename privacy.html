Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The app

Window switching runs on your Mac. WindowGlide has no account or analytics and does not send window titles, thumbnails or usage data anywhere. Window thumbnails are kept in memory only while the switcher is open and are never saved.

When you activate, the app sends the purchase code and a WindowGlide-specific hash derived from the Mac's hardware identifier to our license service. The service stores the order and that hash to enforce one Mac per purchase. The original hardware identifier is not sent. Once activated, the app verifies the signed activation on your Mac and does not contact the service for routine use.

Update checks

WindowGlide checks for updates by downloading an update list from GitHub (github.io / github.com). Like any web request, this exposes your IP address and app version to GitHub. You can turn automatic checks off in the update prompt.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Paddle.com as Merchant of Record. Paddle processes your name, email address, billing country and payment details under Paddle's privacy policy. For online delivery, our service checks the order number and a token stored in your checkout tab. The license key does not contain your email address. If you contact support, we use your email and order number to help with the purchase.
